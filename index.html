Our story

The heart behind the stitches

For our mother, every loop of yarn is a story of resilience and love. Her journey began decades ago in the vibrant streets of Amritsar, Punjab. As the eldest sibling, she stepped up with a quiet strength, using her hands to help provide for her family. What started as a necessary skill soon blossomed into a lifelong passion that has traveled with her across oceans.

Now, in the quiet, snowy winters of Canada, those same hands haven't slowed down. While she once created out of a sense of responsibility to her parents and siblings, and later as a devoted wife and mother, she now creates for the purest reason of all: herself.

This website is a gallery of that journey — a celebration of a woman who gave everything to her family and is now weaving her own dreams, one stitch at a time.

Ask us anything
How long does a handmade order take?
Most pieces are made to order and are ready in approximately 2–3 weeks. If you need something for a specific date, send us a message before ordering and we’ll confirm what’s possible.
Can I request different colours?
Yes. Choose “Custom” in the colour menu and leave a note, or use the contact form for a more detailed colour plan. Reference photos can be attached to both product and custom-order requests.
Do you ship outside Brampton?
Yes. Shipping is available across Canada and internationally. Final shipping costs and applicable taxes are calculated during secure checkout.
Will my piece look exactly like the photo?
Every piece is handmade, so small variations in stitch, colour placement, and dimensions are part of its character. The overall design and palette will stay true to the piece you selected.
How should I care for crochet pieces?
Gentle hand washing in cool water is best. Reshape while damp, dry flat, and avoid hanging the piece while wet so the fibres keep their form.

How to love it back

treat it kindly, it'll last forever

🛁

Cool hand wash

A gentle dip in cool water with a drop of wool-soak. No twisting — just a sleepy soak.

☁️

Lay flat to dry

Pat in a towel, then let it nap flat on a sweater rack. Sunshine is fine, just not direct.

🧺

Fold, don't hang

Hangers stretch shoulders sad. Fold loosely and tuck it into the drawer with a lavender sprig.
